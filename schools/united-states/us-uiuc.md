## 5. 面向金融数学本科生的项目选择提示

- **量化研究、衍生品定价与金融工程：** 优先比较 `us-uiuc-p001`（MSFE）的随机建模与 practicum、`us-uiuc-p002`（MSF）中量化/衍生品的选修组合。MSFE 项目技术定向更集中，MSF 对金融业务方向覆盖更宽。[S01](https://catalog.illinois.edu/graduate/bus_engineering/financial-engineering-ms/) [S08](https://giesbusiness.illinois.edu/msf/curriculum)
- **金融风险与保险精算：** 比较 `us-uiuc-p006`（PARM）的预测分析、风险管理课程与 `us-uiuc-p007`（Actuarial MS）的精算考试衔接。金融数学学生应根据是否以专业精算资格为目标，而不是按学校排名选。[S19](https://catalog.illinois.edu/graduate/las/predictive-analytics-risk-management-ms/) [S24](https://catalog.illinois.edu/graduate/las/actuarial-science-ms/)
- **统计与机器学习：** `us-uiuc-p004`（Statistics General）更适合重理论与统计建模，`us-uiuc-p005`（Analytics）更强调编程、数据管理和行业项目。选择时结合对贝叶斯、时间序列、R/Python 与数据工程的侧重。[S16](https://catalog.illinois.edu/graduate/las/statistics-ms/) [S17](https://catalog.illinois.edu/graduate/las/statistics-ms/analytics/)
- **商业分析与金融数据应用：** `us-uiuc-p003`（MSBA on-campus）有实际 business practicum，重数据库、可视化和业务表达；适合金融机构数据分析、风控数据、商业决策，而非以随机微积分为主的岗位。[S11](https://giesbusiness.illinois.edu/academics/graduate/business-analytics/curriculum)
- **算法开发与金融科技工程：** `us-uiuc-p009`（Urbana campus MCS）课程计算密度更高，若欠缺数据结构、算法与软件项目经历，先补 CS 先修再申请；`us-uiuc-p008`（Applied Math）适合偏优化、运筹及建模。[S28](https://siebelschool.illinois.edu/academics/graduate/professional-mcs/app-info) [S25](https://catalog.illinois.edu/graduate/las/applied-mathematics-ms/)
- **金融经济学与经济数据科学：** `us-uiuc-p010`（EMAIDS）突出经济建模/计量与 AI；`us-uiuc-p011`（MSPE）重经济学基础、金融经济学与政策计量，课程目标与纯金融工程明显不同。[S31](https://catalog.illinois.edu/graduate/las/economic-modeling-ai-data-science-ms/) [S34](https://economics.illinois.edu/academics/master-science-policy-economics/program/curriculum/mspe-courses)
- **资金与回报口径：** 费用差异既受学制影响，也受按学分与按学期计费影响；不要把 USD 23,550/学期与另一项目全学制 USD 57,906 当成同一单位比较。MSFE 的“已报告美国职位平均工资+奖金”及 MSF/MSBA 的“六个月 successful career outcomes”**统计口径不同**，不适合作跨项目薪资/就业率排名。[S05](https://msfe.illinois.edu/admissions/tuition) [S13](https://giesbusiness.illinois.edu/academics/graduate/business-analytics/tuition-fees) [S06](https://msfe.illinois.edu/current-students/placement-information) [S10](https://giesbusiness.illinois.edu/msf/career-outcomes) [S14](https://giesbusiness.illinois.edu/academics/graduate/business-analytics/msba-career-outcomes)
